# Code of Conduct

LocalAlgo contributors are expected to make participation respectful, safe,
and welcoming. Be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people, accept
constructive feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or the
publication of another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should be fair,
proportionate, and explained privately when practical.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. If no private contact is
listed yet, ask for one in a public issue without including sensitive details.
